# Security Policy

## 🛡️ Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it **privately**.

You can do this by:

* Using GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting feature (recommended)
* Emailing us at: **[security@yourproject.com](mailto:security@yourproject.com)**

⚠️ Please do **NOT** create a public GitHub issue for security vulnerabilities, as this may put other users at risk.

---

## 🎯 What to Include

When reporting a vulnerability, please include:

* A clear description of the issue
* Steps to reproduce the bug
* Potential impact
* Screenshots or proof-of-concept (if applicable)

---

## ⏱️ Response Time

We aim to respond to reports within a few days.
Fix times may vary depending on severity and complexity.

---

## 💰 Rewards

We offer rewards for valid vulnerability reports:

* Eligible reports may receive **1 year of our paid plan**
* In some cases, rewards may be exchanged for an equivalent cash value

Rewards are granted at our discretion based on:

* Severity
* Impact
* Quality of the report

---

## ⚖️ Responsible Disclosure

We ask that you:

* Do not publicly disclose the vulnerability until we have fixed it
* Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is necessary to demonstrate it
* Do not access or modify other users’ data

We appreciate responsible disclosure and will work with you to resolve issues quickly.

---

## 🙌 Thanks

Thank you for helping keep this project secure!
